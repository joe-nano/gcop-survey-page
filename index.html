gCOP audit survey

An audit survey for people taking part in COP31, run by Imperial College London (Institute of Global Health Innovation). It asks how health records, the energy used by digital health and preparing health systems for climate change should fit into the UN climate talks.

  • About 12 minutes.
  • Anonymous: no names or email addresses are recorded. Results are published only for groups of 10 or more.
  • Voluntary: you can stop at any time, and withdraw later with your code, without contacting us.

Withdraw your answers

Enter your code on the withdrawal form. You do not need to contact anyone. If the withdrawal form is not working, email your code to jdavids@ic.ac.uk with "Withdraw" in the subject: this tells us your email address, but we delete your answers without reading them, keep only the code and date in our deletion log, and delete your email. Withdrawals received by 23:59 UK time on 4 December 2026 are removed from the final results.
